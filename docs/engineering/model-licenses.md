## Meshy source provenance (A73)

Not every Meshy model in Tervain was generated by the project. Many of the supplied models were downloaded from the Meshy Community and made by other creators. A72 had classified every Meshy source as Meshy Pro paid-plan output; A73 corrects that. Each Meshy source in the ledger now carries a `provenance` record with exactly one classification and the evidence for it:

| Classification | Meaning | Sources | Public files |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `ael-generated-meshy-pro` | Generated by the project through its own Meshy API calls under Meshy Pro, with task ids recorded | 20 | 19 |
| `meshy-community-download` | Downloaded from the Meshy Community, made by another creator; carries `creator` (or "creator not recorded") and `listingUrl` | 0 | 0 |
| `unresolved` | Supplied as a file; who generated it, or whether it came from the Community, is not recorded | 54 | 88 |

Evidence per group:

- **Furniture (17 sources, `furniture-*`)**: project-generated. Preview and refine task ids, prompt and date from the project's own text-to-3D calls are recorded in the [furniture manifest](../../public/models/furniture/manifest.json) (tooling in `tools/meshy-furniture/`).
- **Resident rigs (`resident-rigs`)**: project-generated. Each rig file records its automatic-rigging task id (ledger `generatedRigs`; tooling in `tools/meshy-rig/`). Each rig was made from a supplied resident model, which keeps its own unresolved provenance.
- **Resident motion (`resident-motion`)**: project-generated. Each clip's task id and date are in [clips.json](../../public/models/npcs/motion/clips.json). The clips were made on a rig of a supplied resident (`npc-1004193030`), which keeps its own provenance.
- **Wanderer body (`wanderer-meshy71`)**: project-generated. Ael's account generated it under Meshy Pro on 9 October 2026 (multi-image to 3D, meshy-7.1, task id `01a120c8-22b6-72d4-8c60-2b2451b7edfd`) from renders of the previous wanderer, whose model keeps its own unresolved provenance; the shipped file also carries the A45 skeleton and clips (`wanderer-animated`). Fit and hashes: [hero record](main-hero-assets.json), `tools/hero/rework-hero.mjs`.
- **Supplied models (54 sources)**: unresolved. Two wanderer sources, 15 residents, 19 animals, 15 trees, the solitary pine, the ancient guardian and the rock pile. Their records name the owner as supplier only. No Meshy task id from the project's own generation, Community listing URL, creator name, or author/generator metadata is recorded for any of them; the original files are not in the repository, and the shipped GLBs embed only Tervain preparation metadata (`asset.generator` is a Blender or Tervain tool; no `copyright` or author `extras`). A `Meshy_AI_<name>_<timestamp>_<stage>.glb` filename is what Meshy's export produces for any account and for Community downloads alike, so it does not show who generated a model.

No source is classified as a Community download yet, because no creator or listing is recorded for any of them; none is classified by guesswork. `tools/model-ledger/meshy.mjs` holds the evidence tables (`COMMUNITY_SOURCES`, `AEL_GENERATED_SUPPLIED`); adding a confirmed entry there and running `node tools/model-ledger/classify-meshy.mjs` moves a source out of `unresolved` and updates its derived files.

### Meshy's terms

The [Meshy terms of use](https://www.meshy.ai/terms-of-use) (Last Updated: September 19, 2026) set the branch by origin:

- **Section 3.3 (Community License)**, quoted: "You agree that, if You upload, post, submit, or otherwise make available any image or other content on the Meshy Community page (collectively, "Community Content"), such Community Content is licensed to the public and other users of the Meshy Community page as follows: (a) Customer Output, including 3D models, is licensed under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication (the "CC0 License"); and (b) all other Community Content, including images You upload, is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license (the "CC BY-NC 4.0 License")." Section 3.3 also grants Meshy a perpetual, irrevocable licence to Community Content for running and commercializing its services.
- **Section 3.2** (Ownership of User Content; Rights Granted to Meshy): customers on a paid Meshy plan may keep their User Content private, and, as between Meshy and those customers and to the extent possible under applicable law, they own their Customer Output. Free-plan output is owned by Meshy and made available under **CC BY 4.0** with Meshy credit.
- **Section 3.1**: Meshy owns the Service; its Service Assets are licensed only to the extent incorporated into and necessary to use the Customer Output.
- **Section 2.9**: Meshy may use non-Enterprise customers' inputs and outputs to train, validate, test or improve its Services.
- **Section 7.2** (Disclaimers): Customer Output is provided as is and may be inaccurate; Meshy does not guarantee that it is unique or unlike existing 3D models, and it may not qualify for intellectual-property protection.

So a **Community 3D model is CC0 1.0**: no attribution is required, but Tervain credits its creator wherever known. **CC BY-NC 4.0 is a non-commercial licence**: any Community content other than a 3D model (for example an image uploaded with a listing) requires attribution and may not be used commercially. No asset is recorded under CC BY-NC 4.0; if any is found among the supplied sources it must be attributed and flagged as non-commercial. Project-generated output follows section 3.2 (paid plan).

Each Meshy source's `license` follows its classification: `meshy-paid-plan-output` (project-generated), `meshy-community-cc0` with `spdx: "CC0-1.0"`, `creator` and `listingUrl` (Community), or `unresolved-meshy-provenance` with `creator: "creator not recorded"` (unresolved). The ledger's `serviceRules` holds the terms text.

No classification here is a commercial-clearance certificate. **Commercial clearance is unsettled** until the unresolved sources are confirmed. The classifications do not clear third-party inputs, trademark or publicity rights, and do not cover what is not Meshy output:

- The animated hero source contains **Mixamo-named skeleton and animation content**. Its separate source rights are not independently established; the ledger keeps them under `separateRights` with `evidenceStatus: "pending-source-rights"` on the `wanderer-animated` source.
- The historical Warpkeep archive keeps **LicenseRef-Warpkeep-Provenance-Required** (below).
- Material refinement with other tools, original Tervain additions and code-generated geometry keep their own records.

Credit in the distributed static page: **Original-game imported models created with Meshy; furniture, rigs and motion generated for Tervain; many supplied models downloaded from the Meshy Community, made by other creators (creator not recorded until confirmed); adapted for Tervain.**

The record scripts write this classification for the entries they produce (`tools/meshy-rig/record.mjs`, `tools/meshy-furniture/record.mjs`, `tools/hero/record.mjs`), from shared values in `tools/model-ledger/meshy.mjs`; `node tools/model-ledger/classify-meshy.mjs` applies it to the whole ledger and is idempotent.

## Existing CC freedoms remain intact

Project-generated paid-plan output is not a Creative Commons grant. A source confirmed as a Meshy Community download is CC0 1.0 (and any non-model Community content CC BY-NC 4.0). These rules apply to every source confirmed under a CC license.

If a specific source is confirmed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en), preserve supplied creator/copyright/license/disclaimer notices and a practicable source URI; identify modifications and link the license. Retain earlier modification notices. Do not add recipient restrictions or imply endorsement. If a source is confirmed [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), this ledger imposes no attribution or noncommercial condition on its CC0-covered rights; its creator is still credited where known. If any content is confirmed [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en), attribute it and keep it out of commercial use.

An actual per-source CC license takes precedence over general repository media wording for the rights it grants. Pending evidence elsewhere is not an additional restriction on existing CC rights. The project's [source-code license scope](../../LICENSE-SCOPE.md) remains separate from model content. CC does not automatically clear trademark, privacy/publicity or third-party input rights.

Native terrain, buildings, props, grass, foliage additions and menu geometry also originate from Tervain code. Existing software ports retain their separate software notices. This GLB ledger does not assign a new media license to generated output or the approved Hegemony artwork.

The separate Gothic 3 study/reconstruction routes are **outside the original Tervain asset library**. Their proprietary derivatives are not classified CC, and this ledger records no general rights-holder redistribution or commercial-release clearance. Preserve their [separate notice](../../assets/gothic3/NOTICE.md) and the existing license-scope exclusions rather than listing them as original Tervain models.

The [Ael / Lyrics workflow credit](menu-grove-score.md#studied-workflow-and-attribution) is a recorded **CC BY 4.0 software/workflow reference**, not an imported 3D asset. Its source, author and license links remain in that record.

## Verification and maintenance

The ledger's `auditedModelRevision` identifies the model snapshot; later documentation or credit changes do not validate different binaries. Recompute every listed file's bytes/SHA-256 against its existing source record when a model changes, add new source and modification records, and retain actual license notices. Reconcile the static credits page and JSON counts together. Published model delivery from an immutable build commit does not change the source license.

Before claiming that every model's rights are resolved, confirm the provenance of every unresolved Meshy source, establish the animated hero's Mixamo-named content and the Warpkeep per-set provenance, confirm required creator/source/license/change notices, and verify the distributed credits. Source provenance, permission evidence and a compliance claim are separate findings.
