# Instructions that step aside, and quiet sound captions (A124, 0.0.14)

Ael's playtest 4: the bow's instructions ("Right click to aim · hold Left click, then release to shoot") stayed on
screen the whole time the bow was carried, and captions such as "[Bowstring draws taut]" read like debug output.

- **The bow's instructions** now show for seven seconds each time the bow is taken up, the first three times in a
  session, and then not at all. The reticle and the draw bar under it are unchanged.
- **A sound's caption** is a quiet aside under the scene: no brackets, no box or gold bar, slightly smaller, with a
  shadow to read against the world, fading out over its three seconds. Spoken lines keep their captions as they were.
  High contrast gives sound captions their dark backing again. Captions stay on by default, for speech and sound alike.
