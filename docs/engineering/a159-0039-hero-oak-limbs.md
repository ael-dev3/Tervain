# A159: 0.0.39, hero oaks: real limbs, lobed crowns, bark; grass and animal findings

## Hero oaks against Gothic 3

- Gothic 3's broadleaf trees (the outdoor passage of the 5 October study, plates 0665 to 0750) are a few leafy masses hung on crooked, forked limbs, with sky between them; from below, the limbs carry sprays of leaves. Tervain's hero oaks (42 trees, generated by tools/tree-gen) read as round, even crowns, cauliflower masses, on wicker-patterned trunks.
- With their leaves hidden, the old hero oaks' wood was a spiky vase: ten limbs left the bole within three metres of each other, every limb and bough a straight cone to a point (their turns were bounded to 0.3 rad a step), and the near model draws no twigs. The even crown hid it; any gap showed spikes.
- The bark's wide Worley plates, outlined by near-black furrows and raised as geometry, read as basket weave on the 1.85 m bole of hero-oak-b.

## Change

- Skeleton (tools/tree-gen/lib/skeleton.mjs): six great limbs (four lateral, two leaders) instead of ten, spreading wider (52 to 72 degrees off the bole), turning at elbows (a 17 to 32 degree turn at about a quarter of their steps, still bounded so none hooks back) and sagging more at their ends; boughs grow on a limb's outer half only, so the inner arm is a clean bare limb; fork limbs keep their girth longer. Near limbs are eight-sided. Roots draw from their own random stream, so reshaping the crown does not move them.
- Bark (tools/tree-gen/lib/bark.mjs): plates about twice as fine and three times taller than wide, narrower and shallower furrows, less plate-to-plate contrast.
- Crowns (canopy.ts `CROWN_LOBES`): a hero oak's crown gathers its clumps into five to eight lobes centred on the outer branch tips and the source leaves farthest apart, a few clumps left between them. The lobes are chosen once from the near crown and handed to the middle crown, so shadows match. Wood is trimmed only where it stands bare at the crown's shell, never a limb inside it. Other trees keep the even crown (A134): lobes on the supplied oaks would have left one twig root 0.14 m from its wood.
- Budget: the middle models' leaf clusters are capped (1,750), because the game rebuilds a middle crown in proportion to them. Per template, in game (wood plus leaves), every level is at or under 0.0.38:

## Evidence

- Canopy audit (LOD 0, gap 0.75, reach 1.5 m), all 337 trees: 0 detached clumps, 0 detached cards.
- Real-play walks toward two hero oak groups (12 s each, fixed 1/60 s steps, wind running, 1470×956 at the game's pixel ratio), 0.0.38 and 0.0.39 on the same paths: limbs now show between lobed crowns. In the upper 45% of the frame (the crowns), pixels changing 40 or more levels between frames fall 24% and 26%, 60 or more 27% and 30%; crown brightness rises about 2 levels where sky shows between lobes.
- From below, the crooked limbs carry the leaf clumps with sky between them, as in Gothic 3's plate 0708.

## Grass: shimmer and white specks

- White specks: the masked grass walk (every frame drawn with the 0.0.35 tip cap off, on, and as a grass mask) in four lights: low sun behind, low sun ahead (backlit), noon with the sun behind, noon toward it. With the cap as shipped, grass pixels at 180 or more, near-white pixels (200 or more, little colour) and flashes are 0 in all four. Without it, noon with the sun behind would show 141,180 near-white grass pixels over 120 frames.
- The remaining shimmer is blade geometry: blades under a pixel wide alias as they move, over darker ground. Two fixes were measured and not taken:
  - Widening sub-pixel blades to 1.2 px with alpha to coverage: blade jumps 2 to 17% fewer across the four lights (up to 7% unmasked) but 8% more GPU time at a grass-heavy pose (3.48 → 3.77 ms), because alpha to coverage costs the dense near level its early depth rejection; on the far level alone, no gain (0 to 0.9%) for 1.8%.
  - A lighter ground under near grass (×1.4): 35% fewer large jumps at low sun behind, but 83% more at noon toward the sun, where the blades are darker than the ground; the meadow 3 to 6% brighter.

## Animals: sole contacts

- The delivered animals' paw weights are 0.35 (Float32 0.3499999940395355), and sole sampling required more than 0.35, so no animal samples its paws. In normal play (19 animals, 20 s): one animal floats 3 to 8 cm in 31 of its 80 samples and a paw sinks more than 3 cm in 6% of moments. With the threshold made inclusive, floating goes to 0 and sinking to 9 moments, but the per-frame correction makes roots bob about 22 times more (second difference of height 0.018 → 0.394 mm a frame, worst 0.099 → 1.253) and the land-wildlife update costs about 0.55 ms more a frame. Not taken; a smoothed, cheaper version is the next step.
