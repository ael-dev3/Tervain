# 0.0.16 — crowns, grass, residents and the Air cuts

Builds from playtest 6 and the 0.0.15 playtest clip. The hosted build is at https://ael-dev3.github.io/Tervain/; the
title and pause screens print its commit. Checked by the play gate (desktop and mac profiles) and the playtest visual
gate at dusk, with clips at each playtest spot (net-mender, wooded slope, camera at a trunk, grove, giant tree, meadow,
fence, hearth, sign).

## Trees and foliage

- Leaf clumps hang from the wood: outer clumps sit on grown, tapered twigs, lone clumps join the crown mass, and leaf
  islands left by cut-out transparency are moved onto the boughs for every tree at load. Crowns keep their full width
  and stay within their triangle budget. The canopy audit now judges opaque leaf texels: floating islands 578 → 0 near,
  384 → 0 at middle distance.
- Wood inside a crown is lit as bark in shade instead of reading black.
- Firs and far pines are lit: their needle colour was a third as bright as the broadleaf leaves', so conifers read
  near black at dusk.

## Grass

- Seed heads, petals, pale tips and sheen fade where a blade is under a pixel or two wide, and the near and far grass
  hand over blade by blade instead of narrowing together; blades also turn toward the view smoothly. The band of
  flashing specks at the hand-over is gone (dusk meadow sparkle 0.0035 → 0.0009 on the capture, 0.047 → 0.0025 on
  the gate).

## Residents, hero and camera

- Residents keep their faces on their skulls: the rig had bound most of several faces to the shoulders, so a moving
  head left its face behind (the fisher's "missing head"). Head tilt is limited and hands stay out of the torso.
- The hero dithers out as the camera is pressed against him, so its lens never shows his hollow inside.
- Loosing the first arrow no longer stalls a frame.

## Light and performance

- The sun's shadow fades out over the outer band of its map, so its square edge no longer follows the player.
- Leaves are shaded once per pixel (a depth pre-pass), leaf noise is baked, the shadow pass writes depth only, and
  off-screen trees cast only where their shadow can reach the view: GPU median on the gate machine about 9.9 → 7.1–7.7
  ms. See [the Air cuts](../../perf/AIR-CUTS.md); the GPU is still over [the Mac budget](../../perf/MAC-BUDGET.md).
- Many allocation and repeated-work cuts across grass, wildlife, habitat, residents, skinning and collision.
