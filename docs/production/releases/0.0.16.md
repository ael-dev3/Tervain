# 0.0.16 — shadow edge and Air cuts

Builds from playtest 6 (a MacBook Air: steady 60 fps at the highest settings, less heat). The hosted build is at
https://ael-dev3.github.io/Tervain/; the title and pause screens print its commit. Checked by the play gate (desktop and
mac profiles) and the playtest visual gate before release.

## Light and shade

- The sun's shadow fades out over the outer band of its map, so the bright square that followed the player across the
  ground is gone (the shadow-boundary diagnostic finds no sharp edge samples; 0.0.15 had about 28%).

## Performance

- Leaves are shaded once per pixel: a depth pre-pass draws the foliage's cut-out first, then only the nearest leaf
  is lit (GPU median on the benchmark route about 9.9 → 7.1–7.7 ms, p95 about 16 → 10 ms on the gate machine).
- Leaf masses read a baked noise volume instead of computing noise per pixel; the shadow pass writes depth only.
- Trees off screen cast shadows only where their shadow can reach the view.
- Grass skips tiles and blades that cannot be seen, and shares work across catch-up updates; window captures and
  animals reuse work they used to repeat; the hero's turn and foot steps reuse their scratch objects.
- See [the Air cuts](../../perf/AIR-CUTS.md) for measurements and the cuts still to come; the budget is in
  [the Mac budget](../../perf/MAC-BUDGET.md).

## Grass

- Blades turn smoothly toward the view instead of flipping where they pass edge-on ([A138](../../engineering/a138-grass-facing.md)).
  The sparkle in motion is not yet fixed; it remains open.

## Still open from playtest 6

Grass sparkle in motion, detached leaf clumps at crown edges, the grass draw-distance edge and image quality,
village-pan stutter, jaggies, uneven lighting, and the Mac budget (the GPU is still about 5 ms over it).
