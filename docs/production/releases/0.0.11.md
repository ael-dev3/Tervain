The version remains `0.0.x`.

## Changes

- **Water:** moving/rotating/zooming cameras refresh the planar reflection every rendered pose; its image and projection stay paired. Stationary animation keeps the existing 10/15 Hz cadence. Refraction blends valid scene depths continuously and recognizes actual clear depth. Derivative-aware water detail and highlights address subpixel shimmer. Visible swell, depth absorption, shoreline damping, finite source rocks and the Low/Reduced Effects fallback remain.
- **Running:** the fresh 100-point meter drains **0.75 points/s** during actual running travel, reaching the sprint threshold after approximately **132.7 seconds** and leaving about **10 points after 120 seconds**. Authored walk/run speeds remain **1.65 / 5.85 m/s**. Jump/dodge/combat costs stay separate; blocking/no-direction input does not spend running stamina. Recovery hysteresis prevents run/walk pulsing at empty.
- **Travel and camera:** finite contacts protect camera movement and recoil; falling support is checked across the travelled interval. Low rock steps, walkable facets, solid walls and the graded 92-tread lighthouse route keep their existing authority. This does not add unrestricted climbing, root-motion-driven movement or per-foot IK.
- **Input and records:** control conflicts require a deliberate release/re-press, browser/modal events stay out of gameplay, stored settings are validated, and map pointer handling tolerates unavailable capture. New-game slots remain empty; real inventory items, bindings, equipment and the personal map pin retain compatible saves.
- **Audio state:** buffering, seeking, native pause and output-context events now describe the actual score stream. A delayed pause cannot erase a terminal decoder error or mark an already-resumed stream paused. Unexpected output suspension exposes the title's Play control, which can recover a browser-held resume request through a deliberate gesture. There is no automatic retry loop. The same audio element, source position, score clock, headroom and mix remain.
- **Save lifecycle:** backgrounding during a graphics rebuild or failed replacement preserves the previous save instead of snapshotting a retired physics world. Saving resumes after the replacement becomes usable.

The approved title/pause vigil, single Hegemony banner, score-led grove, ships and music remain. The owner-supplied hero and pine binaries are unchanged. Attached gameplay tree/leaf sway stays paused; detached falling leaves remain. Nine physical supplies retain F lift/drop and R throw with pose-only saves. The A49 NPC expansion adds selected imported derivatives under the source record below; dependencies and workflow triggers remain unchanged. Separate `/gothic3/` and `/gothic3-local/` work is preserved outside this original-game patch.
