# 0.0.9 follow-up — rounded woodland air and falling leaves

The owner identified a small pale square in the woodland screenshot and requested gently falling leaves ([A43](../../decisions.md)). This follow-up starts from published main `07ff2610969deb62ffd0095761dca6c5f066ce72` and stays **0.0.9**. The approved hero, source tree forms, draw-continuity work, grounding and canonical collisions remain.

## Floating square

Woodland pollen used an untextured `PointsMaterial`, which rasterized each particle as a square. Its 0.12 m particles match the pale rectangle in the supplied canopy view. A texture-free radial `gl_PointCoord` mask now discards the square corners and softens the particle edge before stock alpha testing. Stock perspective size attenuation, depth testing, fog, tone mapping and gradual habitat opacity remain. No new texture, generated image or imported asset is involved.

## Detached leaves

Detached-leaf motion is separate from the approved static tree geometry. Trunks, branches and attached crowns retain paused sway. Leaf origins come from actual oak, birch and orchard leaf cards after the tree's source yaw, uniform scale and grounded position; the pine, fir, shorepine, dead tree and scrub families do not emit broad leaves. The effect is sparse and local rather than a leaf storm across the coast.

Each detached leaf is a folded, pointed, lit **10-triangle** native mesh with a raised central vein and subtle vertex colors. It is **0.16–0.24 m** long and falls at **0.24–0.42 m/s**, with bounded breeze drift, sideslip and slow flutter. Sources are seeded by stable tree identity and staged at different phases rather than emitted as one synchronized wave. High has up to two streams per tree, capped at **256**; Medium and Low have one, capped at **160 / 80**. These are implementation budgets, not a new ecological simulation.

A fixed-size instanced mesh uses per-instance opacity to fade over **30–55 m**, at release and at settlement. Tiny shapes retain depth testing and stock light/fog/tone treatment, do not write depth or cast shadows, and use a fixed contact pose before disappearing and returning invisibly to their source. Terrain clearance follows the full rotated leaf envelope on hills. This is cosmetic animation with terrain contact, not branch, trunk, character or item collision simulation; the canonical movement colliders are untouched. The module owns its geometry/material/instance resources and never changes or disposes a source tree geometry.

The module advances its own elapsed-delta clock, so Reduced Motion freezes descent, drift and flutter exactly even while the game clock advances. Animated updates run before flora's idle-camera culling shortcut; trees can remain static while detached leaves keep moving. No new image, GLB, texture, external source, dependency or asset-repository upload is needed.
