# 0.0.15 — trees, shade and homes

The builds from the playtests of 0.0.14 (playtests 5 and 6). The hosted build is at https://ael-dev3.github.io/Tervain/;
the title and pause screens print its commit. Every change below is checked on that hosted build by the play gate and
by the playtest visual gate (`tools/visual-gate.mjs`): clips and stills at the playtest's spots, scored against
[the rubric](../../playtests/5/RUBRIC.md) and posted on the pinned playtest issue.

## Trees and foliage

- Broadleaf crowns are rebuilt from clumps of small leaf clusters on a procedural atlas: dense, rounded masses instead
  of flat cut-out cards; leaf edges are soft and keep their coverage at a distance. See
  [A133](../../engineering/a133-volumetric-crowns.md) and [A134](../../engineering/a134-cluster-canopies.md).
- Every broadleaf's root flare meets its slope (the placement audit's floating roots: 13 → 0).
- Broadleaf trunks have bark with relief.

## Light and shade

- Shade keeps a sky fill, and sun shadows soften with their distance to the caster: a long house shadow at a low sun
  is a soft, readable shade instead of a black slab, and the ground under trees by the stream is no longer near black.
  See [A137](../../engineering/a137-shade-fill.md).

## Houses and village

- Hearths are built into a wall, with an open fire, a flickering light, a mantel and a pot; rooms have timber posts,
  braces and joists, a plank wainscot, crowded shelves and believable jar sizes, and every house its own layout. See
  [A135](../../engineering/a135-gothic-interiors.md).
- Each house has its trade's yard and its own paint and roof details. See
  [A132](../../engineering/a132-village-exteriors.md).
- The inn sign hangs from its bracket; grass no longer grows through fence rails or haystacks.
- The fields lie on graded ground instead of lumps of bare earth.

## Hero and play

- The fence vault works from a stride before the rail at any crossing angle, walking or running.
- The bow is carried string side to the back, steeper and smaller, clear of the shoulder; its string is a lit cord.
- An empty quiver warns once, not in a stack of identical messages.

## Performance

- On High, Retina screens draw at 1.5 pixels per CSS pixel instead of 2 (44% fewer pixels); grass, trees, leaves,
  water spray, animal hits and skeletons skip unchanged uploads and invisible geometry. The MacBook Air budget and the
  measurements behind it are in [MAC-BUDGET](../../perf/MAC-BUDGET.md); the work toward it continues.
