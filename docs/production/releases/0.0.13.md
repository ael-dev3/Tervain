# 0.0.13 — hunting candidate

The woodland prototype now supports bow hunting and animated carcass harvesting. Launched arrows move at 65 m/s and resolve against head/body zones and blockers: one head hit or two body hits kills an animal, independently of draw time. Skin requires a valid close flank stance at the decoded carcass chest, runs for 3.2 seconds and grants one hide plus species-dependent meat once on completion. Moving cancels even on the final animation frame. Wounds, fixed corpse poses and harvested state persist in format-1 saves.

The early trail supplies contain a bow, knife and 24 arrows. One hide buys six replacement arrows, bounded at 40 carried arrows. Updated aim/draw controls, guidance, blood effects with Reduced Effects support and six packaged ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 clips complete the loop. See [the hunting guide](../../engineering/hunting-0.0.13.md), [model provenance](../../engineering/animal-assets.json) and [hunting sound provenance](../../engineering/hunting-sounds.json).
