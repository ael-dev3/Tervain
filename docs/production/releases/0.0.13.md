# 0.0.13 — woodland animals and bow hunting

## Behavior

All nineteen supplied animals join the original Tervain world: tiger, lion,
two bears, three wolves, three cats, two dogs, three boars and four deer.
Each has a distinct model and habitat placement. The complete rendered animal
is below **50,000 triangles**, including every mesh. The pack totals
**687,852 triangles** and **136,787,804 bytes**; the largest is the tiger at
**47,155 triangles**.

Models retain source geometry, UVs and embedded surface textures, with
reductions where needed. Each has an original 29-bone quadruped skeleton and
articulated clips. Standing animals have Idle, Walk, Run, Alert and Call;
boars and deer also graze. The seated cat keeps its source posture and has
Idle, Alert, Call, Groom and Sleep. The deer mount keeps its saddle and tack,
without riding controls. It now rests with the inland caravan, away from the hunter camp and wild stag, and cannot be shot, harvested or included in hunting objectives. Old saves restore accidentally killed or skinned mounts without resetting other hunting progress. The tiger's fused paw contact was locally separated
and closed while preserving the surrounding source surfaces. Deer antlers
follow the head rather than deforming with the torso.

Movement follows terrain-resolved travel and the measured clip stride.
Animals avoid water, steep footing and solid scenery, with checked routes
and player/resident/cargo contacts. Feet use source-specific support samples.
All nineteen remain in the population on every graphics preset, with expanded
animated bounds and ordinary frustum culling; there is no distance eviction.
Cats and dogs stay near settlements, forest animals occupy woodland, and the
big cats inhabit the warmer eastern woods.

The reviewed hunting gameplay from PR 34 is reconciled with this complete
pack. A bow, skinning knife and 24-arrow bundle rest on a solid, braced counter beside the woodland
approach. Its readable sign is mounted behind the tabletop with its post behind the board. Rowan Vale, using the existing 34,220-triangle ranger model, welcomes nearby travellers with an offline Eleven v4 line. Equip the bow; hold right click or L to aim, hold left click or J
to draw, then release to shoot. Moving arrows use swept contacts with the
current posed animal triangles and terrain/scenery cover. One head hit or
two body hits brings down a wild animal. **Thirteen animals are huntable;
the six protected companions include three cats, two dogs and the saddled deer.**

Carcasses collapse from their visible pose, settle on the local ground and
provide a checked knife approach. Press V or Skin nearby with the knife to
begin a 3.2-second kneeling action. Movement cancels it without rewards.
Completion grants a hide and species-dependent meat once. One hide buys six
arrows at the supplies, capped at 40 carried arrows. Wounds, death, harvest
and corpse location persist in compatible format-1 saves. Unfinished actions
and arrow flights are transient. Wildlife does not respawn in this prototype.

The prior hero, trees, coastal world, menu, quests, inventory, map, hotbar and
separate study routes remain. Tervain stays in the **0.0.x** development stage.

## Audio

Sixteen isolated animal calls, two per species, were generated using
ElevenLabs **Sound Effects v2** (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`). The supplied
account lists Eleven v4 as a speech model; the sound-effect endpoint rejected
that identifier. Its v3 identifier also returned an unknown-model error.
This release does not claim v4-generated animal sounds.

Calls originate at the animated mouth during the actual Call gesture, with
species-dependent hearing ranges, distance filtering, staggered cooldowns
and at most three simultaneous voices. Source MP3s are retained; mono Opus
and AAC derivatives trim quiet edges, remove DC and fade both ends.
The 16 calls total **39.197 seconds**; both browser formats together use
**712,339 bytes**. All 32 derivatives decode to finite, non-silent mono audio.
True peaks range from **−13.45 to −3.77 dBTP**. The development sound tool
provides individual auditions. Signal checks do not constitute listening
acceptance on every device.

Six short hunting effects from the reviewed PR cover bow draw/release,
flesh/ground impacts and skinning/start-completion. They use Effects/Master;
animal calls use Ambience/Master. Menus, panels, hidden tabs, death, mute,
rebuilds and disposal cancel playback and invalidate pending decodes. The
client contains no provider credential and makes no generation request.

## Water rework, 6 October 2026

Every body of water now comes from one model that answers where water stands and how it moves: the sea, the main
stream, the mill and quarry races, the spring's fall and its pool. The sea's swell is refracted over the actual bed,
shoals and breaks at its depth limit, and runs up the sand as swash. Wind waves ride on top. Streams fall downstream,
stay within their banks, spill at a low bank and run as a film down cascades. The mill race's bed now falls gently
from its intake, and the mill wheel stands half a metre further over its flat bed so the paddles turn in the race's
water.

Drawn water uses:

- Fresnel reflection of the coast and the captured sky;
- refraction absorbed over the real path, with caustics on the submerged ground;
- surf foam only where waves actually break;
- flow-driven ripples and foam inland;
- an interactive ripple field and spray near the player.

Barrels and crates float, bob, drift and splash. The hero wades more slowly with depth, swims in water too deep to
stand in, and is carried by currents. He cannot fight, guard, shoot, jump or work in deep water. A swimmer's camera may
look up from under the surface, through absorbing water with caustics and a muffled world. Arrows splash, slow and
float. The earlier layered sea and channel meshes are removed.

Seventeen water sounds were generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2 from original descriptions: six beds and eleven
sources for one-shots. They are placed from the same model, and each crest's crash is heard as it breaks. Their prompts
and hashes are in the [audio source record](../../engineering/world-audio-assets.json). Generation-time subscription
evidence was not recorded with them, so their commercial clearance is not established. Existing world, voice and music
files are unchanged.

The [water record](../../engineering/water-0.0.13.md) describes the model, drawing, interaction, sound, presets and
validation. The combined source passes TypeScript, **1,689 tests across 167 files** and the regular production build. Captures of
the strand, rocks, bay, ford, streams, spring, swimming and the view under water were reviewed. A listening review,
reference-hardware frame rates, required remote CI, deployment and the served revision remain separate checks.

## Grass, 6 October 2026

The grass is rebuilt as an engine of its own (A61). Each clump's blades are built on the GPU from its seed:

- leaves, flowering stems with seed heads, and a few flowers;
- bent along curves that keep their length;
- lit as thin leaves that pass light from behind.

A gust field carried downwind sends visible bands of bending grass across a meadow, with each blade fluttering inside
them. The hero, residents, bandits, animals and rolling cargo push the grass aside and lay it down. The trail closes
over several seconds. The realm streams two levels of detail that hand over without a seam. The terrain's grass
layers are slightly darker beneath, so meadows read as sward. The 0.0.10 folded tufts, their texture and patch
material are removed.

On the title screen the heath is the centre of the scene:

- three levels around the fixed camera, backlit by the low sun;
- a sea wind that swells with the score;
- the standard flying and the wind's sound rising with the gusts;
- a stag grazing its way across, the spirits skimming low, and the pointer brushing through the grass;
- seed fluff drifting on the same wind.

The stag is the wildlife's own model, loaded once. The cart track is narrowed to two ruts with grass between.

The [grass record](../../engineering/grass-0.0.13.md) describes the blades, wind, interaction, presets, cost and
validation. On the development machine the grass costs about 0.1 ms of GPU time a frame in a realm meadow and on the
title screen. The combined source passes TypeScript, **2,097 tests across 208 files** and the regular production build. Title
captures on every preset, frame sequences of the wind and the pointer, and a walk through a realm meadow with its trail

## Foliage, 7 October 2026

The trees and their leaves are reworked (A62). Every tree and shrub now moves in the realm's one wind, the same gust
field the grass shows, so a gust crossing a meadow moves on into the woods:

- trunks lean downwind and sway at a period set by their size, saplings faster than old trees;
- branches swing coherently with their reach from the trunk;
- leaves flutter inside the turbulence;
- shadows move with them, so the dappled light on the ground shifts.

Leaves are lit as leaves. Crowns shade as rounded volumes instead of card by card, light passes through leaves seen
against the sun or the sky, crown interiors darken, and over-saturated greens are drawn back. Ferns and low shrubs
sway too and part around the hero, residents and animals, reading the grass's own trample field. An arrow in a trunk
shakes the tree, a sapling visibly and an old oak hardly at all, and knocks a few leaves loose; falling leaves drift
downwind. The world's wind sound rises as each gust passes. On the title screen the ancient tree's crown sways in the
sea wind and glows against the sunset, while its carved trunk, door and lanterns stay still.

The frame is lighter. Tree shadows come from shadow-only casters: only trees inside the sun's shadow volume, with the
lighter source models away from the camera; the colour pass no longer draws trees the camera cannot see, and empty
levels leave the render lists. The low decorative shrubs fade to their own lighter models. High still draws full
source trees in view at every distance. No model, texture, sound or music file changes.

The [foliage record](../../engineering/foliage-0.0.13.md) describes the wind, light, touch, shadows, cost and

## Residents, 7 October 2026

The residents are reworked (A63). Their joints bend where their own bodies do: each elbow and knee moves onto the
model's real limb. Shoulders blend instead of tearing, capes and aprons hang from the body instead of swinging with an
arm, and skirts and hems share both legs, so a stride no longer splits them. Joints blend as rigid motions, so elbows,
knees and hips keep their volume when they bend.

Arms hang clear of hips, skirts and coats. Hands land on the hips, clasp before the belt, rest behind the back or fold
across the chest on each figure's own body; a gesture a body cannot make cleanly is left out of that figure's routine,
and the head scratch is retired. Residents sit on the actual benches with their palms on their thighs, instead of
sinking into the planks. Sel Anrit writes in a ledger with a quill, Rowan Vale rests both hands on the counter and now
and then lifts an arrow to sight along it, Pell Dunmore strikes a chisel with a hammer at the rock, and Mara Venn,
Sister Edda Sorn and Bess Corran work with a measuring rod.

Skin, wool, leather and steel shade as what they are, with a fine grain close up and crisper detail. Covered cloth
layers that a deep bend used to push through aprons and skirts, showing as dark shards, now stay behind their cover. No
model, texture, sound or music file changes; every complete actor stays within its budget.

The [resident record](../../engineering/residents-0.0.13.md) describes the joints, skin, poses, seats, tools, surfaces,

## Weathered look, 8 October 2026

The world looks less glossy and more weathered (A67). The surfaces that dress the buildings and the props built in code
are baked at four times their former resolution, 1024 px. They show rubble bedded in dirty mortar, peeling and
damp-stained render, split boards with rusting nails, sooted and lichened roof tiles, old thatch and slate, cobbles in
mud, furrowed bark, weathered rock and tarnished bronze. Walls darken towards the ground with rising damp and splashed
mud. The sky light no longer gives rough surfaces a polished sheen. The wanderer, the residents' skin and leather, the
furniture, the animals and the metal props are matte. The grade turns greens to olive and mutes the sky, keeps the reds
and golds, and adds contrast in the lights, a vignette and grain. Medium and Low decode the same textures at 512 and
