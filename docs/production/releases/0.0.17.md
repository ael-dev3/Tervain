# 0.0.17 — Continue lands on the ground

A hotfix to 0.0.16. The hosted build is at https://ael-dev3.github.io/Tervain/; the title and pause screens print its
commit.

## Fixed

- **Continue from a save could rebuild the hero beneath the land.** The view showed the world's underside, with trees
  and village lights hanging in the dark, and the hero was not drawn though he could walk.
  - A loaded height is never beneath the ground.
  - Water counts as swimming water only where its surface is well above the ground.
  - Restored cargo can no longer lower the safe standing height.
- **If he is ever beneath the land for a moment while not swimming,** he is set back on the nearest safe ground with the
  camera.

## Checked

- `tools/continue-gate.mjs` saves, reloads the page and takes Continue twice. The first save is an ordinary one. The
  second has its height pushed 3 m under the ground, which is what an old save looks like after the land changed.
- Both rounds must bring the hero back on his feet, drawn, with the camera above the land.
