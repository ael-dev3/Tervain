# 0.0.9 follow-up — walkable Lantern Point and grounded world

The owner requested a proper lighthouse approach and entry, believable world scale, firmly planted trees and a regular third-person camera after reviewing the published `0.0.9` landscape ([A41](../../decisions.md)). Version stays **0.0.9**. This follow-up starts from the published main source `1b6be3f9a95109ff7a60c880f52d85059bee8ed1`; it preserves the original forest, approved hero, menu and existing item/save systems.

## Ground contact and movement

The earlier cliff track was painted onto the ungraded headland, and the lighthouse's solid collision volumes prevented entry. The new route grades the actual rendered/collided terrain rather than placing an invisible ramp through rock. Tower and keeper-room openings, floors and wall boundaries share authored dimensions with collision and standing support. Exterior spiral and gallery access remain. The dirt track is **132.22 m** long and **3.2 m** wide; dense centre/side-strip samples measure a maximum local terrain gradient of **28.02%**. Tower and keeper entrances clear **2.1 m** and **2.0 m** respectively, with real ceilings and floors. These are current construction measurements, not a new world-design standard.

The earlier tree origin sat six centimetres below the soil at its centre. On a slope this left the downhill root ends in the air. Grounding now considers the actual basal wood faces of all near/middle/far variants, their instance yaw/scale and the terrain's rendered grid/diagonal intersections. A single downward translation embeds the base; source vertices, UVs, proportions and normals are preserved. Collision clearance includes the resulting burial depth. Broadleaf families and shrubs use the same contact rule. Gameplay tree and leaf motion remains paused. Flora counts remain **482 / 547 / 612** for Low / Medium / High, including **259 / 271 / 281** imported conifers and **376** canonical tree obstacles. Across High placements, median burial is **17 cm**, with maximum tree burial **6.50%** of authored height. Eleven conifer collision footprints expand to contain branches newly lowered into the player-clearance slab. The three GLBs retain their pinned hashes.

## Scale and camera

One world unit is one metre; the approved Wanderer stands **1.899 m** tall. The existing house doors, bench seats, barrels, carts, boats and adult trees are reviewed against that anchor. The exaggerated rugged style remains, while ordinary carried food is no longer enlarged into knee-high props: the apple is about **14 cm**, mushroom **19 cm**, loaf **49 cm**, and gathered herb **60 cm** at their longest extent. These are implementation calibrations, not new lore or an owner-approved universal size standard. Their interaction targets follow their actual placed bounds.

The Rillford fingerboard is **1.85 m** wide and **0.40 m** high at shoulder/eye level, with its lettering, timber post and braces built to those dimensions. The well's thatch hood now rests on its timber crossbeam instead of appearing around its stone ring at ground level. Individual posts, beam and pitched-roof collision protect the camera without turning the low well ring into a tall solid cylinder. House cargo, woodpiles and the bakery oven settle independently across their authored footprints instead of floating at the building's averaged foundation height; adjacent small crates are separated.

The mill keeps its original planked wheel, now supported by a wall-entering shaft and planted outer bearings. Its complete rotating envelope clears the actual riverbed by at least **4.20 cm** across 96 measured phases. Lower paddles meet the actual restored water ribbon under both allocations that turn the wheel; the dry quest state remains dry. The axle and wheel have finite collision volumes matching the assembly.

Default upward mouse/right-stick motion now looks upward. The old input sign was wrong for the camera's elevated-boom pitch convention. Horizontal look and camera-relative movement stay consistent; genuine saved `Invert Y` choices are honored. Upward viewing expands to inspect lighthouse and tree crowns, retaining terrain/scenery obstruction and smooth boom recovery. This adapts the expected third-person feel with original code, rather than claiming an exact copy of Gothic 3's camera implementation.

## Acceptance

The suite walks the complete keeper track through the real controller at 30/60 Hz, enters and leaves both rooms, matches visible floors/ceilings with support and collision, and retains 30/60/120 Hz spiral/gallery coverage. Interior saved positions restore onto their actual floors; solid masonry and arbitrary air hints remain rejected. Geometry audits scan every delivered placement/LOD across graphics presets, preserve all 32 authored routes, check actual prop supports and full wheel rotation, and verify the unchanged source-tree hashes. Expensive per-point assertion allocations were replaced with aggregated maximum errors while preserving the exact scans and thresholds; required checks and workflow triggers stay intact.

Native production-preview review reaches READY with the visible graded trail, open tower and keeper interiors, complete floor/ceiling surfaces, slope-planted pine and oak roots, supported mill assembly and well hood. Native upward/downward mouse drags move the view in the expected direction. Full route traversal, controller-stick semantics, interior-save restoration and exhaustive placement coverage are automated source checks; they are not claimed as a complete human walkthrough or physical-controller session. Screenshots and measured geometry reports are retained separately from the earlier release baseline. The final local candidate shows the prior HEAD `1b6be3f` in its pre-commit debug stamp. The existing Vite large-chunk advisory remains, and no new frame-rate or minimum-device claim is made.
