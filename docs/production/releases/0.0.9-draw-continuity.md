# 0.0.9 follow-up — continuous scenery

The owner requested improved draw distance without visible objects popping in and out ([A42](../../decisions.md)). This follow-up starts from published main revision `6e9faa8a731d722831337de89fcc5a26ec26e79e` and stays **0.0.9**. The approved hero, source-faithful grounded trees, static gameplay foliage, route/collision identities, menu and save systems remain.

## Causes and treatment

Terrain, architectural geometry and merged rock chunks already remain resident, with bounds computed from their actual geometry. The 1,400 m camera far plane covers the playable map. NPC bodies have no distance visibility cutoff. The relevant problems were abrupt tree LOD handoffs, hard smaller-vegetation cutoffs, stale manual culling after camera changes, incomplete ground-cover preload and undersized bounds for displaced grass. A larger far plane alone would not address these.

Trees retain cheap distant silhouettes and change detail through complementary opaque dither coverage rather than a single-frame mesh swap. High blends near/middle over **36–48 m** and middle/far over **116–148 m**; Medium uses **24–36 m** and **104–136 m**; Low retains real middle-detail trunks locally and blends to far cards over **64–96 m**. Far cards remain fully present to **900 m** and fade out by **1,020 m**, where the clear daytime fog already hides them. Coverage is shared by wood, foliage and shadow passes; alpha-tested leaf textures retain their original masks. Conservative bounds encompass every authored LOD, yaw and scale. Off-screen casters are retained against the actual snapped sun-shadow frustum rather than an oversized radial allowance. Camera movement and turns refresh selection promptly; an idle camera tracks slowly moving shadow bounds at up to five updates per second, with immediate refresh for a large sun/focus jump. Approved source GLB geometry, textures, proportions and pinned hashes are unchanged.

Grass now fades over **12–96 m / 10–80 m / 8–64 m** for High / Medium / Low, previously **8–60 m / 6–48 m / 4–34 m**. Stable instance rank controls gradual coverage without collapsing tufts into their anchors. Shared CPU/shader thinning power **2.1** limits the cost of the wider window; the CPU does not submit an extra tail of shader-discarded ranks. A bounded two-tile preload ring builds ahead of movement, while a teleport fills already-visible missing tiles immediately. Conservative tile bounds include scaled blade shapes, wind and player bending. CPU rank selection remains a conservative superset of the shader's visible patches.

Forest-floor pieces retain their authored shapes while gradually fading over **112–208 m / 88–168 m / 64–128 m**, instead of hard disappearance at **128 / 92 / 56 m**. Logs, ferns and shadow casters use the same coverage rule and actual geometric bounds. These distances are current prototype controls, not new lore or a minimum-device performance promise.

Woodland particles fade with local habitat coverage. Lanterns attenuate smoothly and retain a light slot until its outgoing light has faded. Coastal reflection capture follows actual visible sea bounds instead of an arbitrary world-X gate, retaining existing quality and refresh limits. NPC shadows use the renderer's actual shadow-camera culling instead of a separate hard 55 m player-distance switch. Intentional quest state, defeated enemies and sleeping residents retain their behavior.

## Acceptance

**Strict TypeScript, all 828 tests in 79 files (`npm test -- --maxWorkers=4`, 30.89 s), the production build, all three pine integrity checks and `git diff --check` pass.** All 779 relative documentation links resolve. Tests exercise actual source-instance coverage on short camera steps, preserved uniform transforms/root contact, complementary shader coverage and source alpha masks, real off-screen tree/fern casters, idle sun changes and large jumps, visible-tile catch-up on teleports, deformation-safe grass bounds, the complete shader-visible rank prefix, light-slot handoffs, reflection-gate continuity and one-time resource disposal. An earlier fixture run timed out during concurrent build/browser work; the isolated cases and final full suite pass. The all-preset/all-LOD root scan is split into individual cases with its exact assertions retained.

Native production-preview review reaches READY on High, Medium and Low with intact foliage textures, grounded tree shapes and no captured warning/error logs. A native camera drag changes the view immediately, retaining scenery at the screen edge. The built-in landing-to-woodland-to-town route completes. This is camera-route and preset coverage, not a complete human walkthrough or physical-controller test. Pre-commit candidate screenshots/debug reports carry parent stamp `6e9faa8`; hosted acceptance must verify the final deployed revision separately.

The wider vegetation window and overlapping tree detail have a material cost on High. In the native Chromium 154 in-app browser, Apple M5 / ANGLE Metal, at **1422 × 800 CSS pixels and DPR 1.8**, the final route reports **32.60 / 34.00 / 50.00 ms** median / p95 / p99, worst **100.1 ms**, over 2,793 frames. The earlier cached world-contact preview reports **16.70 / 33.30 / 33.80 ms**, worst **83.9 ms**, over 3,924 frames. These are display-limited animation-frame intervals, not GPU timings; the clock is not pinned, the cached baseline embeds its earlier pre-commit `1b6be3f` stamp, and the runs are observational rather than a controlled reference-device qualification. They do not demonstrate a performance improvement. At the fixed woodland view the final grass submission is **8,404 patches / 277,332 triangles**, compared with **6,271 / 206,943** before; these CPU counters include frustum-rejected tiles. Scene/render-pass totals are not unique asset triangle counts. No minimum specification or Steam performance claim is made.

Dither transitions overlap at most two adjacent tree levels; distant trees remain inexpensive. This method softens silhouette changes without inventing new tree shapes, but is not temporal antialiasing: stippled coverage can be visible during transitions, particularly on Low. The wider High grass preload holds about **9.45 MiB** of instance attributes at a grid-origin view versus **2.53 MiB** previously; an entirely visited hysteresis disk has about **14.01 MiB** of attribute capacity. These are deterministic buffer-size calculations, excluding habitat/source geometry and GPU overhead, not measured total memory. The existing Vite large-chunk advisory remains. Publication uses the unchanged PR check and main Pages workflow; final run IDs and hosted revision are retained in delivery evidence after verification.
