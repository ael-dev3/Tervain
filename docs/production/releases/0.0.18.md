# 0.0.18 — clean grass in motion, crowns on their boughs, no pop-in

Builds from the playtest of 0.0.16 (white specks in the grass, floating leaf chunks, pop-in and a muddy distance).
The hosted build is at https://ael-dev3.github.io/Tervain/; the title and pause screens print its commit. Checked by
the play gate, the Continue gate, the dusk video gate at the playtest's spots and the walk metrics, against 0.0.17.

## Grass

- **No more white specks in motion.** With 4× MSAA, a pixel a thin blade only partly covers was shaded at its centre,
  outside the blade, where the blade's colour and lighting overshot their range. Tone mapping turned those values into
  single white (or black) pixels that flickered as the view moved. Blade values are now held to their real range.
  Near-white flicker on the dusk walk: 28 → 0; sparkle 0.85% → 0.26%.
- Flowers tint their blades instead of glinting, and fade out with distance. Dry straw and pale tips fade on blades too
  thin to resolve.
- **Grass reaches further.** It now runs to 120 m (was 82 m). The ground takes on its colour as it thins, so the meadow
  has no edge. The near and far grass hand over blade by blade.

## Trees

- **Every leaf clump hangs within reach of its boughs.** Clumps could chain off one another, up to 4 m from any branch.
  A new detector checks the frames as they are drawn; it cut exposed floating leaf area by 46% on a dusk forest run.
- Tree shadows cross-fade between models instead of switching at a hard distance. Pop events on a 200 m walk through
  village, fields and forest: 1.24 → 0 per metre.
- Firs lose a cyan patch from their needle atlas. At dusk, distant woods stand as a dark treeline instead of pale
  ghosts.

## Rooms and village

- Hearth rooms have framed walls, firewood, pans and herbs by the fire. The inn's sign is a painted board on a
  wrought-iron bracket and sways in the wind.

## Also

- A fence vault no longer passes through loose cargo.
- Collision shapes follow resized animals and carcasses.
- Many allocation and upload cuts.
