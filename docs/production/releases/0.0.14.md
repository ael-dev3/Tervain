# 0.0.14 — houses that are lived in

The first build from Ael's playtest 4 of 0.0.13 (build `2690343`). The hosted build is at
https://ael-dev3.github.io/Tervain/; the title and pause screens print its commit. Every change below is checked on that
hosted build by the play gate (real keyboard and mouse input on a desktop and a Mac Retina profile).

## Playtest 4

- **Interiors (item 1):** each of the six village houses is now a household (weaver, woodcutter, herbalist, hunter's
  family, farmer's family, brewer), with its own furniture and hearth side. Every room is dressed: bread, cheese, jugs and
  candles on the tables, clay pots on the shelves, a cooking pot in the hearth, antlers, a hide and drying herbs on the
  walls, and woven rugs on the floors. Twelve new Meshy props. See [A123](../../engineering/a123-lived-in-houses.md).
- **Fences (item 7):** a fence is a wall to walk into and a vault to cross: jump while facing one within reach. The hero
  no longer glides along the rails, and the camera no longer catches on fences.
  See [A125](../../engineering/a125-fence-vault.md).
- **Main menu (item 11):** the tall black slabs in front of the sea are the camp's standing stones. They now stand below
  the horizon and read as weathered stone. See [A122](../../engineering/a122-menu-stones.md).
- **UI (item 12):** the bow's instructions show for a few seconds when it is taken up, and sound captions are a quiet
  aside rather than bracketed notices. See [A124](../../engineering/a124-quiet-ui.md).
- **Grass (item 5, part):** blades stand in the same light as the ground, spread evenly and a little lighter, instead of
  dark tufts on bare earth. See [A116](../../engineering/a116-grass-root-shade.md),
  [A117](../../engineering/a117-grass-spread.md) and [A121](../../engineering/a121-grass-even.md); reserve grass tiles
  outside the visible band are built gradually (#301).
- **Hero (items 3 and 9, part):** the slung bow swings and bobs with his steps, his arms come in at his sides, and his
  stride fits the ground so his feet stop creeping in stance. See [A118](../../engineering/a118-slung-bow-swing.md),
  [A119](../../engineering/a119-arms-in.md) and [A120](../../engineering/a120-stride-fit.md).

## Also in this build

- The hero no longer flickers between a full-size and a collapsed pose, and the lag and stalls in real play are gone: on
  the hosted build the Mac Retina profile holds a 17.1 ms 95th-percentile frame with no long tasks
  ([A113](../../engineering/a113-hero-flip-and-live-build.md), [A114](../../engineering/a114-real-play-lag.md),
  [A115](../../engineering/a115-real-play-stalls.md)).
- A merge reaches the hosted build in about two minutes, and a newer build reloads itself or offers a reload.
