# Tervain 0.0.10 — detailed woodland and movable supplies

The owner's current goal authorizes finishing and shipping **0.0.10** ([A44](../../decisions.md)). The revision prioritizes source-faithful trees at a distance, better close materials and ground cover, and physical world interaction. It remains in the required `0.0.x` line. The published [0.0.9 release](0.0.9.md) and its [world-contact](0.0.9-world-contact.md), [visibility](0.0.9-draw-continuity.md) and [falling-leaf](0.0.9-falling-leaves.md) follow-ups remain historical evidence, not validation of this combined revision.

## Integrated behavior

- **Complete trees on High:** the exact near/source variant is retained at every visible distance, preserving the approved trunk, roots and crown instead of replacing them with simplified middle geometry or crossed far cards. Grounding and uniform scale remain authoritative. Source GLBs, canonical placement and collision IDs are unchanged. Medium/Low retain lighter distance models. Attached tree sway stays paused; A43's detached falling leaves remain.
- **Close bark:** original metre-scale 1,024 px triplanar colour and relief/roughness add real generated grain, plates and cracks. Source macro colour remains, relief changes shading rather than deforming vertices, and the detail fades with distance. The existing source textures are not relabelled as higher-resolution rebakes.
- **Ground and grass:** High/Medium terrain texture layers rise to 1,024/768 px; Low stays 256 px. Finer generated soil, rounded shingle, strata and leaf litter replace coarse patterns. A 1 m render grid preserves the original 2 m physical planes. Folded blades gain material relief and exact source-height fitting at each root, plus better bounds and obstacle clearance. Daylight and contact-shadow settings improve visibility and grounding.
- **Water pass eligibility:** cached wet-source triangle bounds prevent inland views of shader-discarded dry sea geometry from triggering reflection/composite passes. Actual water geometry, waves, shoreline damping and reflection quality remain intact when sea is visible.
- **Nine physical supplies:** seven barrels and two crates replace their stationary copies. **F lifts/drops; R throws; E retains existing item/inspection behavior.** Cargo falls, tumbles, stacks and collides with finite scenery, source-tree wood and living actors. The held object follows a collidable spring. There is no destructible-world or object-damage system.
- **Travel and saves:** steep terrain no longer has the ordinary walking-slope cutoff; downhill sliding, airborne momentum, ballistic jumps and supported falls are integrated. Existing solid contact, deep water and world boundaries remain. Format-1 saves retain object poses alongside existing progress; velocities and held state are not serialized.

The approved Weathered Wanderer, unarmed start and equipment, original NPC pipeline, inventory/map/pickups, sparse coastal arrival and inland route remain. The title/pause vigil, supplied score, one approved Hegemony banner, ships and score-synchronized grove remain. Conversation windows remain removed. This revision adds no mobile work, new region, Steam integration or desktop package. Physics uses pinned Apache-2.0 Rapier **0.21.0** at **60 Hz**, with original Tervain models and no Valve content. See the [visual engineering handoff](../../engineering/visual-detail-0.0.10.md) and [physics handoff](../../engineering/physics-0.0.10.md).

## Running endurance

The owner requires at least two minutes of default running on stamina ([A47](../../decisions.md)). Sprint drain is now **0.75 points/s** from the fresh 100-point meter instead of 12 points/s. Continuous travel reaches about **132.7 seconds** before the 0.5-point sprint threshold, leaving about **10 points after 120 seconds**. This is uninterrupted exploration running; jumping, dodging and combat still spend their existing separate amounts. Authored running speed remains 5.85 m/s.

Exhaustion uses that same sprint threshold, including a low externally assigned starting balance. Existing recovery above 22 points prevents alternating between running and walking near empty. Only resolved travel above 1 m/s spends running stamina; held sprint with no direction or against an impassable wall does not. Regeneration rates, recovery delays, menu suspension and action costs remain unchanged.
