# Separate Gothic 3 reconstruction

The hosted /gothic3/ route contains selected derivatives of third-party Gothic 3
meshes, textures and placement data from the owner's installed game. They are
not original Tervain assets and have no asserted open-content license. No
copyright transfer or relicensing is implied by the owner's hosting request.

Native sources, generated hashes, conversions and current limitations are
recorded in public/gothic3/source-manifest.json and tools/gothic3/README.md.
Quest/dialogue research sources are in content-provenance.json in this folder.

The offline preparation scripts retain GPL-3.0-only notices and their full
license under tools/gothic3. The separate browser implementation does not
import those scripts. Three.js retains its MIT notice in the published
third-party-notices.txt.
