3D models and their licenses

Original-game imported models were created with Meshy and adapted for Tervain's world. Some were generated for Tervain; many supplied models were downloaded from the Meshy Community and made by other creators. The historical Warpkeep archive has its own source records.

Provenance partly unresolved

Not every Meshy model here was generated by the project. Of the 107 public model files, 88 derive from 54 supplied Meshy source files: many of them were downloaded from the Meshy Community and made by other creators, but which ones, and by whom, is not recorded yet, so all 54 are listed as unresolved (creator not recorded). The residents' 17 rig files, their motion library, the 17 furniture models and the wanderer’s body were generated for Tervain through its own Meshy account (Meshy Pro), with every task id recorded. The animated hero’s Mixamo-named skeleton and animation content keeps a separate, unresolved source-rights record. This page preserves credits and changes; commercial clearance is unsettled until the unresolved sources are confirmed.

How Meshy’s terms apply

Meshy’s terms of use (Last Updated: September 19, 2026) give each Meshy model one of three branches, depending on where it came from:

  • Generated for Tervain under Meshy Pro (section 3.2, paid plans): customers on a paid Meshy plan may keep their content private and, as between Meshy and them and to the extent possible under applicable law, own their Customer Output. Meshy keeps a non-exclusive license to use that content to provide its service. This covers the furniture, the resident rigs, the resident motion and the wanderer’s body.
  • Downloaded from the Meshy Community (section 3.3): “Customer Output, including 3D models, is licensed under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0) 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication”; all other Community content, including uploaded images, is licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0. A Community 3D model is therefore CC0: no attribution is required, but its creator is credited here wherever known.
  • Non-commercial condition: any Community content other than a 3D model (such as an image) is CC BY-NC 4.0, which requires attribution and forbids commercial use. No asset here is recorded under it; if one is found among the supplied sources, it must be attributed and kept out of commercial use.
  • Free plan (section 3.2): Meshy-owned output under CC BY 4.0 with Meshy credit. No source is recorded under it.
  • Sections 3.1 and 7.2: Meshy’s own service assets are licensed only as far as they are part of, and needed to use, the output; output is provided as is, and Meshy does not guarantee that it is unique or unlike existing 3D models.

To confirm

The 54 supplied Meshy sources (two wanderer sources, 15 residents, 19 animals, 15 trees, the solitary pine, the ancient guardian and the rock pile) are unresolved: for each, whether it was generated by Ael under Meshy Pro or downloaded from the Meshy Community, and if so its creator and listing. Their filenames are in the ledger’s toConfirm list. Commercial clearance is unsettled until they are confirmed.

Should any source later be confirmed under a Creative Commons license, its CC freedoms apply in full: for CC BY 4.0, preserve supplied notices, credit as designated, identify modifications and link the license, without added restrictions or implied endorsement; for CC0, this ledger adds no attribution or noncommercial restriction. Unresolved or special-permission records keep their recorded project-use scope and are not relabeled CC.

Actual CC rights take precedence over general repository media wording for the rights they grant. The source-code license does not license these model files or reduce a recipient’s existing CC rights. No Meshy branch clears trademark, publicity/privacy or third-party input or animation rights; they need their own applicable permission.

Historical Warpkeep archive — 209 GLBs

These are byte-exact copies from Warpkeep revision 786c0b2, representing 76 catalog assets. They are archive-only, outside the current original-game model catalog.

The archive manifest records LicenseRef-Warpkeep-Provenance-Required and specific project-use authority, rather than an open-content grant. Thirty-one files retain embedded Ael/Warpkeep copyright text; that metadata is a recorded notice, not independent ownership proof. Per-set terms remain in Warpkeep-Assets. All archive file hashes and immutable source links are included in the JSON ledger.

Separate geometry and study material

Native terrain, buildings, props, grass, custom foliage and menu geometry also originate from Tervain code. Software ports keep their own notices; generated geometry and approved emblem artwork are not assigned a new media license by this ledger.

The separate Gothic 3 study/reconstruction routes contain proprietary game derivatives and are outside the original Tervain asset library. They are not classified CC. This ledger records no general rights-holder redistribution or commercial-release clearance for them; see the separate source notice.

All 297 inventoried files matched their source-record hashes. No embedded CC declaration or external texture/model dependency was found in these GLBs. Metadata and credit alone do not establish the applicable license.
