How the actual source license applies

Meshy’s terms, updated 19 September 2026, distinguish Free output under CC BY 4.0, paid-output terms and Community 3D output under CC0. Other Community content, including uploaded images, uses BY-NC 4.0. The source branch must be established for the specific asset; its filename does not prove that branch.

  • Confirmed CC BY 4.0: preserve supplied creator, copyright, license, disclaimer and practicable source notices; credit Meshy where designated; identify modifications and link the license. Existing CC freedoms remain, without added restrictions or implied endorsement.
  • Confirmed CC0: this ledger adds no attribution or noncommercial restriction to CC0-covered rights. Source credit remains useful provenance.
  • Unresolved or special permission: retain the recorded project-use scope while obtaining actual source evidence. Do not relabel it CC or claim private ownership.

Actual CC rights take precedence over general repository media wording for the rights they grant. The source-code license does not license these model files or reduce a recipient’s existing CC rights. Trademark, publicity/privacy and third-party input or animation rights need their own applicable permission.
