Tervain world audio

Sound effects, ambience and part of the in-world score were generated with ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io) from original Tervain prompts on the free plan. These generations and files containing their derivatives are limited to noncommercial prototype use with required attribution.

Commercial and Steam clearance is blocked until those generations are separately licensed or replaced. Buying a subscription later does not retroactively license free-plan generations. The project software license does not override these asset terms.

Other world music and sounds are original Tervain compositions synthesized in code, without generated audio or source recordings. These independently crafted renders have separate provenance and are not governed by the ElevenLabs free-plan restriction. No open-content license to Tervain's original audio is granted here; mixed files retain the restrictions of generated material they contain. The owner-supplied menu score also retains its own terms.

The repository's audio provenance record identifies each source or crafted render, processing recipe and derivative by hash.
